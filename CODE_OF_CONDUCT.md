# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Expend to be respectful and harassment-free for
everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about code and kind about people.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction.
- Give reproducible reports and actionable review comments.
- Respect privacy. Never post another person's transaction data, backups, or
  screenshots without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, or discussions.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and
other project spaces.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through their GitHub profile. For
security vulnerabilities, use the private reporting path in `SECURITY.md`, not a
public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as
practical.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may ask for a correction, remove content, lock a thread, or restrict
participation. Severe or repeated violations may result in a permanent ban.
